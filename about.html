The Project

Sentinels is an original comic project built around four characters who become something greater than they expected.

The website serves as the project's official digital archive — a place to read the story, explore the characters and interact with different parts of the world surrounding it.

Sentinels began as an idea and grew into a world of its own. The project is being developed as an ongoing comic universe, with the archive evolving alongside the story.

This website is part of that process — a home for the story as it continues to take shape.

Not every part of the Sentinels world is available yet. Some files remain classified, some stories are still being developed, and future volumes will open as the project progresses.
