THE STORY

SOMETHING
HAS CHOSEN THEM.

The Sentinels are not soldiers. They weren't searching for power. And they certainly weren't prepared for what was coming.

Four people. Four colours. Four different abilities. Together, they become something neither they nor the world fully understands.

As their abilities begin to develop, so do the questions surrounding them. Who chose them? Why them? And what happens when something from beyond Earth comes looking for them?
